03 — Keep It Luxurious

Hair Care, The JHAIR Way

Wash Weekly

Sulfate-free shampoo, cool water, always brush before you wet.

Silk Wrap Nightly

Protect the pattern and cut down on tangling while you sleep.

Low Heat

Heat protectant first, styling tools under 350°F for longevity.

Deep Condition

Every 2 weeks to keep the moisture and shine locked in.
